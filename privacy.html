Privacy notice.

Draft for client and legal review · 13 August 2026. This preview explains how information submitted through the Dawson Landscaping website may be handled. Confirm it against the final WordPress, form, analytics and business systems before publication.

Information we collect

When you send a project enquiry, the website may collect your name, phone number, email address, suburb, requested service, preferred timing, approximate budget and project details.

If you choose to upload photos or plans, they may also contain property features, access details or other information visible in the file. Please upload only material you are authorised to share.

How information is used

Enquiry information is used to understand your request, contact you about the project, arrange an appropriate next step and maintain a record of the business conversation.

Website measurement

If analytics or advertising tags are enabled, the website may measure page views, calls, enquiry-button clicks and successful enquiry submissions. Personal information should not be sent to analytics platforms.

Cookies and similar technology

The production website may use essential storage needed for security and forms, plus optional analytics or advertising technology where it is enabled. The final cookie controls and consent wording must match the tools actually installed at launch.

Sharing and storage

Information may be handled by service providers used to host the website, deliver forms, manage enquiries or provide business systems. It should only be retained for as long as reasonably required for the enquiry, service delivery or applicable record-keeping obligations.

Security

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards should be used to protect enquiry information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask Dawson Landscaping to correct or review the personal information associated with your enquiry, request access where applicable or raise a privacy concern. Use the verified phone or email published on the final website. The business should confirm its privacy-contact process before launch.

Updates

This notice may be updated when the website, contact system or business process changes.

Legal review required before production publication.
