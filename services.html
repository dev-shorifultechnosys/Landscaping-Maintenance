Complete project thinking

Decisions that
work together.

Levels affect drainage. Paving changes movement. Plant choices shape watering and maintenance. Considering these relationships early helps create a landscape that feels resolved rather than added together over time.

  • Access, levels and water movement considered first
  • Ground preparation matched to the selected finish
  • Appropriate equipment chosen around access and scope
  • Hardscape, turf, planting and watering coordinated
  • Site tidy and practical handover included in the final stage
