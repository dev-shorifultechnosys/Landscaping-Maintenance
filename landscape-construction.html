Construction scope

The pieces of a complete build.

Every site is different. The final scope can combine the elements required for the agreed design and property conditions.

01

Site preparation

Access planning, clearing, excavation and groundwork appropriate to the defined project area.

02

Levels & structure

Practical transitions, edging, retaining and garden structure aligned with movement and drainage.

03

Paving & paths

Usable outdoor surfaces and connections installed around clear falls, edges and finished levels.

04

Soil & planting

Preparation and planting arranged to create form, screening, shade and seasonal interest.

05

Turf & irrigation

Lawn and watering components coordinated with planting zones and future maintenance needs.

06

Finishing details

Mulch, clean edges, site tidy, walkthrough and practical information for the new landscape.
