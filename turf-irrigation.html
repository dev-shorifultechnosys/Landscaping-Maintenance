Install for the whole landscape

Prepare the ground.
Plan the water.

Turf performance begins with the ground below it, while irrigation needs to respond to the lawn, garden beds and established planting. Coordinating preparation, pipework and installation helps each zone begin from a clearer foundation.

  • Existing ground, access and establishment needs reviewed
  • Soil preparation completed before the turf finish
  • Pipe routes and watering zones related to the layout
  • Lawn and garden requirements separated where suitable
  • Local restrictions and ongoing responsibility confirmed before use
