Hardscape with a purpose

More than a
finished surface.

Paving and limestone define movement, levels and usable outdoor space. Their shape and placement can make a garden feel calmer, clearer and easier to use.

Dawson considers how surfaces meet lawns, planting, entries and entertaining areas. The goal is not simply to add hardscape, but to use it where it improves flow, structure and everyday function.

Preparation before finish

Build the base.
Then refine the surface.

A clean finished surface depends on the preparation below it. Ground conditions, excavation depth, base material, compaction, falls and edges are considered around the agreed application.

  • Access and existing ground conditions reviewed
  • Base preparation matched to the selected surface
  • Compaction, falls and adjoining levels checked
  • Edges, joints and transitions finished carefully
  • Surrounding planting used to soften built elements
