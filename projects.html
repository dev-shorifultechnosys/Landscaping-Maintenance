Project thinking

A successful landscape is not only attractive on completion day. It should create clearer movement, more useful zones and a realistic relationship between materials, planting, water and maintenance.

Every project is shaped around the property, the way the space will be used and the level of ongoing care that suits the owner. Explore the decisions behind each landscape direction below.
