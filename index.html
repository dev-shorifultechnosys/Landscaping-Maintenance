How the work comes together

Prepared below.
Resolved above.

A finished landscape depends on what happens before the final lawn, planting or paving is visible. Site access, ground preparation, levels and the order of work are considered around the agreed scope.

  • Access and existing site conditions reviewed first
  • Ground preparation matched to the selected finish
  • Levels, water movement and transitions considered together
  • Appropriate equipment selected around access and project scope
  • Planting, turf and surfaces finished with a clear handover
